I've completed a comprehensive machine learning analysis on your concrete datasets to predict compressive strength under different exposure conditions. This analysis provides actionable insights that will help you optimize concrete mix design and predict long-term performance with confidence.

## What I Analyzed

### Given Data
I worked with your three different exposure conditions datasets - NaCl (salt), Water, and Na2SO4 (sodium sulfate). Each dataset contained 14 data points measured over 30-420 days, with 13 input parameters including cement composition, fiber properties, and exposure time. My goal was to predict compressive strength accurately.

### Key Findings from Given Data
After analyzing Given datasets, I discovered some interesting patterns:
1. **NaCl Exposure**: Your concrete strength decreases over time (51.46 → 47.83 MPa)
2. **Water Exposure**: Your concrete strength increases over time (52.43 → 61.56 MPa) 
3. **Na2SO4 Exposure**: Your concrete shows significant strength increase (53.71 → 66.89 MPa)

These findings will be crucial for your long-term planning and quality control processes.
